## Scope of the search

The search used targeted queries for adversarial manipulation of explanations in ICS/SCADA/CPS intrusion detection, then checked the above primary research records, publisher pages, and full arXiv text. The 2025 XAI-manipulation preprint and the 2026 IDS/IoT explanation-drift studies use generic/tabular or network settings. The ICML 2026 TSEF paper establishes dual-target attacks on time-series classifiers, while the SSRN preprint abstract explicitly includes SWaT and WADI and claims both explanation-aware attacks and defenses; its full text was not accessible, so its exact overlap remains unresolved and is a blocking novelty gate for the proposed direction. Separate process-systems papers study TEP adversarial detector robustness, and the September 2026 *Sensors* article studies TEP fault-versus-measurement-integrity triage with abstention. The search did not yet verify a study that specifically optimizes post-hoc explanation displacement while preserving the detector alarm, under a simulator-grounded and declared sensor capability set, and evaluates a state/measurement consistency defense. That narrow distinction is provisional pending inspection of the SSRN preprint and a broader citation-chain search.

This does **not** prove that no study at this intersection exists. It means the current review has found closely adjacent work and one inaccessible preprint whose abstract may substantially overlap the proposed direction. Any gap statement should describe the specific sources and access limits; do not claim “first” or treat the gap as verified until that preprint and the citation network are reviewed.

## Implications for candidate directions

1. Do not claim as new that adversarial inputs can manipulate SHAP/LIME/gradient explanations, that predictive accuracy and explanation stability differ, or that dual-target attacks can exploit time-series explanation coupling. These are demonstrated in generic security or time-series settings.
2. A potentially distinct CPS question is whether an attacker with a declared measurement-layer capability set can steer a detector's feature-level explanation while preserving its alarm output, and whether an independently measured process-state/observation consistency signal can flag the explanation as unreliable. This remains a hypothesis, not a novelty claim; it must be compared against the IDS explanation attacks, the TEP attack/triage studies, and especially the inaccessible SWaT/WADI SSRN preprint above.
3. Historical HAI, BATADAL, and Data4Cyber telemetry can test retrospective explanation behavior but does not by itself establish physical reachability. A simulator or validated, version-matched process model is required for such a claim.
4. Keep explanation stability and adversarial robustness as evaluation dimensions. They are not standalone contributions here.

## Reproducibility and reviewer checks required before a method claim

- Compare with explanation-manipulation attacks from the 2025 preprint and the strongest applicable black-box/gradient attacks from the 2026 preprints. Record exact implementation or justified adaptation differences.
- Define attacker access, capability channels, query budget, and whether the target is prediction evasion, explanation displacement, or both.
- Use a process simulator with a disclosed model version, parameters, initial conditions, and validation evidence; preserve the underlying physical state while modeling sensor spoofing separately from actuator tampering.
- Report detector output change, explanation displacement, target-feature/group transfer, attack success rate, and runtime jointly. Do not compress the result into a single robustness score.
- Hold out complete simulated attack scenarios and fit all normalizers, thresholds, feature groups, and detector parameters without test labels.
- Treat published telemetry-only results as external validity checks, not as physical-reachability evidence.

## Simulator feasibility note

The [TEP-Rust project](https://github.com/jkitchin/tep-rust) reports a pure-Rust port derived from Downs and Vogel's original Fortran, a live-oracle differential check of a 48-hour closed-loop trace, deterministic scenarios, and Python bindings installed from source. It exposes measurements and manipulated variables in the public interface. These are promising source-level feasibility indicators for separating plant state, measurements, and controls; they are project-maintainer claims, not an independent peer-reviewed validation. Before adopting it, pin a commit and license, reproduce the required API path locally, document the exact simulator variant and scenario semantics, and independently compare a small set of outputs with the upstream Fortran oracle or published references. The separate TEP adversarial-detection studies use dataset releases that may not match this simulator configuration, so direct numeric comparisons require schema and scenario reconciliation.
